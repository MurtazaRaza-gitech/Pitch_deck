The Problem

We consume sugar without always realizing how much.

Three everyday frictions make sugar one of the hardest things to manage.

Hidden Sugar

Sugar hides in everyday foods and drinks people never expect it to be in.

Limited Awareness

Most people simply don't know how much sugar they take in across a single day.

Difficult Tracking

Traditional food logging is slow, tedious, and hard to keep up for more than a few days.
